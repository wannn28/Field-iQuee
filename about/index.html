About the shop

A small shop, on paper.

This page is the longer version of the home-page note. It does not add a founding year or a client count, because those were never part of the demonstration.

Designing rooms. Keeping houses.

Field & Timber, in this story, remodels houses people already live in. No spec homes and no catalog. The shop on the page is four carpenters, a notebook, and a schedule that is written before anyone orders a door.

Drawings come first. Site work does not start the day a contract is signed. In the sample jobs the lead time is five weeks, usually for doors or drawings, and the usual drive is twenty-five minutes.

The address on the profile, 18 Bogard Street, and the number (843) 555-0148 are part of the demo. They are not a working shop. A visit request opens WhatsApp with a draft message.

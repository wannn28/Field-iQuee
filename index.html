Questions

Is Field & Timber a real shop?

No. It is a fictional Charleston remodeler, built as a portfolio demonstration. You cannot hire it, and the address is not a storefront.

Are the figures prices?

No. $86,400, $214,000, and $468,000 are sample numbers so the page has something to show. They are not bids and not sale prices.

Is the house a real property?

No. The house is a CC0 model, and the kitchen pieces are a CC0 furniture kit. Neither is a photograph of a Charleston job.

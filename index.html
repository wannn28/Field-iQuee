What the shop takes on

Three kinds of work, written as scopes.

    Scope
  2. 02

    Historic restoration

    Scope
  3. 03

    Whole-home

    Systems, two baths, a kitchen, and a porch on houses that still want to look like 1948 when the scaffold comes down.

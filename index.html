Our services

From Survey to Finish

Kitchens

Galleys opened toward the garden. Soapstone on the counters, painted inset doors, and a heart-pine floor patched instead of replaced.

Historic Restoration

Piazzas, sash, and plaster that stays. Posts and rails are drawn before anyone orders cypress. The street front is repaired, not redesigned.

Whole-Home

Systems first, and the facade left alone. Panel, heat, and baths sit in the middle of the schedule. Paint and the porch come last.

Baths

Scoped with a kitchen or a whole home, not as its own contract here. Beadboard, a sink that drains, and brass left to go dull.

About us

Designing Rooms.
Keeping Houses.

A small shop with a written schedule, in this demonstration. Heart pine, cypress, limewash, soapstone, and brass that is allowed to go dull. No spec homes and no catalog.

Drawings come first. Site work does not start the day a contract is signed. In the sample jobs the lead time is five weeks, usually for doors, and the usual drive is twenty-five minutes.

Heart pine is patched, not replaced. Cypress is for porch posts and decking. Soapstone is for counters. Brass is unlacquered and allowed to go dull. The address and the phone on this site are part of the demonstration.
